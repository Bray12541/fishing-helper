Fishing Helper 4.5

- Update card with release notes, progress, speed and cancellation.
- Download in the background; optionally install after fishing and cleanup stop.
- Retry interrupted downloads up to three times; verify size, checksum and executable format.
- Skip a version or remind tomorrow; keep preferences after restart.
- Stable and Beta channels, with Stable rejecting Beta releases.
- Startup health acknowledgement and rollback for updates installed by 4.5 or later.
- Optional Windows publisher signature validation and certificate pinning.

This release is unsigned: a trusted code-signing certificate has not been configured. Enabling required signatures blocks unsigned updates. The first upgrade from 4.4 uses 4.4's original installer; startup-health rollback applies to later upgrades initiated by 4.5 or newer.
